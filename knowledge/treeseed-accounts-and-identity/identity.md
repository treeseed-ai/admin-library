Your account identity connects your sign-in credentials to the profile other people use to recognize your work.

## Public identity

Your username is the permanent address of your public profile. Your name, image, headline, website, location, areas of knowledge, and profile summary are optional public identity fields.

Use a profile image and summary that make your knowledge work recognizable without exposing private contact or employment information.

## Regional settings

The time zone setting controls how dates and times are displayed throughout the authenticated application. It does not change the underlying recorded instant.

## Email addresses

Email addresses are private account credentials. New addresses remain pending until their confirmation link is used. Keep at least one verified address so account recovery remains possible.
