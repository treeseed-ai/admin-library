The feedback collection is restricted to platform administrators because reports can include private account, team, route, and screenshot context.

Use search and filters to locate related reports. Opening a record shows its verified submission context, technical details, attachment privacy state, and immutable status history.

Team administrators and market administrators do not receive feedback access.
