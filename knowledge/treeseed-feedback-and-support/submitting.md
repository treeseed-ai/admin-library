Choose the closest feedback type and describe what you expected, what happened, and how it affected your work.

TreeSeed derives your signed-in identity and validates your team or project access when the report is submitted. You cannot change those values in the feedback panel. Administrators see your verified email only when you explicitly allow follow-up.

Never include passwords, provider tokens, private keys, or other secrets in the message.
