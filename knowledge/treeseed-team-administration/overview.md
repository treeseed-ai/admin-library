The overview is the fastest route to the team context you use most often.

## Your access

Effective permissions come from your current team role. Expand the role description to see the same human-readable responsibilities used in membership management.

## Owned domains

Projects, Services, Capacity, Knowledge, and other domains keep their own mutation interfaces. The overview summarizes their current state and links to the canonical page.

Recent security activity identifies the human actor, meaningful operation, useful details, and your time-zone-adjusted timestamp. Machine identifiers remain forensic details rather than primary labels.
