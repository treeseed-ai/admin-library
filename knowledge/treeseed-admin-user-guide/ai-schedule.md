Always on and Weekdays are draft scheduling choices. Enter an IANA time zone such as Europe/London so the intended local calendar is explicit.

Saving a schedule does not start or stop cloud machines. Activation must first qualify scheduling, safe draining and checkpoint handling. The current draft form does not configure workday start/end hours. Do not assume a saved schedule is enforcing availability or cost limits.
